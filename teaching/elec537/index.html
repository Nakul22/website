Course Description. This course focuses on building intelligent mobile systems that sense, communicate, and make decisions under tight resource constraints like limited energy, compute, and bandwidth. We will explore wireless communication, embedded sensing, and machine learning techniques for mobile applications. Topics include wireless localization, sensor fusion, on-device AI, energy harvesting, wireless imaging, and emerging techniques like 3D mapping and neural radiance fields.

Prerequisites: The course is open for graduate (PhD/Master's) and senior undergraduate students. Prior experience in embedded systems, wireless communication, and machine learning is recommended but not required.

Topics.

  • Fundamentals of Signal Processing: Analyzing sensor data using Fourier transforms and time-frequency analysis.
  • Wireless Communication & Ranging: Understanding wireless signals for communication, localization, ranging, and angle estimation.
  • Advanced Wireless Imaging: Creating images from wireless signals using physics-informed AI, Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF), and diffusion models.
  • On-Device AI & Optimization (TinyML): Implementing and optimizing efficient machine learning models for resource-constrained devices.
  • Energy Harvesting & Intermittent Systems: Designing battery-free devices that compute using harvested energy.
  • Acoustic Sensing Systems: Leveraging sound for tracking, ranging, and gesture recognition using techniques like Doppler and beamforming.
  • Next-Generation Wireless: Investigating the future of sensing with 5G, millimeter-wave, Terahertz, and smart surfaces.
  • Sensing for Robotics & AR/VR: Enabling perception for autonomous robots and immersive experiences in AR/VR.
  • Mobile Health Sensing: Using smartphones and wearables to monitor physiological signals and create digital biomarkers.
  • Security and Privacy: Building secure systems with privacy-by-design, and exploring side-channel atatcks.
  • Large Language Models (LLMs): Integrating AI, LLMs and chatbots into intelligent mobile applications.

Grading Breakdown.

  • Assignments (25%) - 2-3 assignments covering wireless sensing, localization, and embedded AI
  • Midterm (25%) - One open-book in-class midterm exam
  • Presentation (10%) - Student paper presentation
